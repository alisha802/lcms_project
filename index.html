ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Built for Modern Legal Excellence

LCMS combines litigation management, intelligent scheduling, bar-verified attorney
matching, and direct voice memo dictation into a unified, secure web platform.

End-to-End Case Lifecycle

Manage legal cases from the initial client registration to court proceedings and final case decisions, all in one place.

Bar-Verified Advocates

Every lawyer's license is checked and verified by the admin before they can provide services to clients.

Voice Problem Dictation

Clients can record their legal problems using their browser, helping lawyers better understand their issues before consultations or court hearings.

CORE CAPABILITIES

Comprehensive Legal Solutions

Everything attorneys, corporate legal counsel, and individual clients need to navigate complex court proceedings with clarity and confidence.

Appointment Booking

Clients can request appointments with their chosen lawyers by selecting a date and time, while lawyers can accept or reject requests and manage their schedules.

Lawyer Search & Profile

Clients can search for lawyers by specialization and view their profiles, including qualifications, experience, and bar registration details, to choose the right one.

Lawyer Verification

Administrators verify lawyers registration details and credentials before approving their profiles, so only verified lawyers can offer services on the platform.

Document Management

Clients and lawyers can securely upload, store, and manage case documents in one place for easy access anytime..

Case Tracking

Clients can track their case progress, view the current status and receive updates from their assigned lawyers.

Notification & Reminder

Users get timely notifications about appointments, court hearings, meetings and case updates so they never miss important information..
